##AI Work Assistant
**
A unified productivity platform designed for students, recent graduates, and modern professionals to eliminate administrative friction and streamline daily workflows.

**Overview**

AI Work Assistant brings together essential productivity tasks into a single interface. By combining an AI chat feature, a structured meeting notes summarizer, and a web grounded research assistant, the platform removes the need to juggle fragmented and unintegrated tools.

**Key Features**

* **AI Chat Feature:** Act as a flexible conversational partner for brainstorming, troubleshooting, and answering questions in real time right alongside your workspace.
* **Meeting Notes Summarizer:** Extract core takeaways, key decisions, assigned action items, and deadlines from messy notes or full transcripts instantly.
* **AI Research Assistant:** Surface accurate, web grounded insights and structured research briefs without the hassle of sifting through endless search results.

**Target Audience**

* **Students:** Managing heavy lecture notes, academic research papers, and communication with professors.
* **Recent Graduates:** Navigating the job search, handling interview follow ups, and building professional portfolios.
* **Professionals:** Streamlining daily administrative tasks, summarizing syncs, and managing workplace communications.
